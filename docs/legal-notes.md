# Questions for Crypto-Specialized Counsel

**This checklist is for preparing a legal consultation. It is not legal advice and does not determine whether any activity is lawful. Obtain advice from qualified counsel in each relevant jurisdiction before marketing, selling, or distributing tokens.**

- [ ] How might MKA, the proposed public sale, staking tiers, subscription discounts, and buyback policy be classified under applicable securities, commodities, payments, consumer-protection, or other laws?
- [ ] Which jurisdictions may be targeted, and which must be excluded or geo-restricted? What evidence and controls are appropriate?
- [ ] What KYC, AML, sanctions-screening, recordkeeping, and suspicious-activity obligations apply to the project, any sale, and service providers?
- [ ] What restrictions apply to public advertising, social posts, influencers, referral programs, performance statements, and communications about token utility or market activity?
- [ ] What disclosures, sale terms, refund rules, eligibility terms, risk warnings, and consumer-protection obligations are required?
- [ ] How should subscription discounts, in-app payments, access tiers, and any staking arrangement be structured and described?
- [ ] What tax reporting, collection, withholding, and accounting obligations may apply to the project and participants?
- [ ] What privacy, data-retention, and cross-border data-transfer requirements apply to KYC and product data?
- [ ] What legal duties apply to treasury control, liquidity provision, token burns, and any proposed use of subscription revenue?
- [ ] Which licenses, registrations, filings, or local counsel opinions may be required before launch?
- [ ] What contractual terms and disclaimers should cover third-party data, signal content, availability, outages, and user responsibility?
- [ ] What incident-notification, consumer-remediation, and regulator-contact procedures should be documented?
