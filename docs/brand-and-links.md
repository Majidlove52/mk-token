## Anti-scam guidance

- Use only the contract address published on the verified official website and cross-check it against the verified BscScan page.
- The team will never send the first direct message to solicit funds or provide wallet support.
- The team will never ask for a seed phrase, private key, wallet recovery phrase, or remote access to a device.
- Do not trust unsolicited links, lookalike accounts, unofficial contract addresses, or claims of guaranteed returns or listings.
- If an account or message appears suspicious, do not interact with links or attachments. Report it through the official support channel after verifying that channel from an official source.
