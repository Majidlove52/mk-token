# MK Alpha (MKA) Litepaper

**Draft summary:** details marked as placeholders are not commitments and require review before publication.

## What is MK Alpha?

MK Alpha is a proposed ecosystem of trading-related software: indicators, scanners, and signal applications. MKA is a fixed-supply utility token intended to support optional access and payment features as those products are developed. Product features and availability remain subject to technical and legal review.

## The product direction

The project aims to make related market tools easier to discover and access through a shared product layer. Indicators can display selected market measures; scanners can filter data against chosen criteria; signal applications can present generated or third-party signals with methodology and risk context. These tools provide information, not individualized financial advice. They do not remove the risks of trading.

## Proposed MKA utility

Potential uses include subscription discounts, staking tiers that determine access levels, in-app payments, and token-gated tools. These are proposals rather than current contract features. Any staking tier is an access mechanism, not a promise of yield or token rewards. Separate service terms and legal review will be required before these functions launch.

## Supply and allocation

MKA has a fixed supply of **1,000,000,000 tokens**, minted once to the treasury at deployment. The token contract has no post-deployment mint function. The following allocation is proposed and is not enforced by the contract:

| Allocation | Share | Tokens |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Public sale | 30% | 300,000,000 |
| DEX liquidity | 20% | 200,000,000 |
| Team | 15% | 150,000,000 |
| Ecosystem and rewards | 15% | 150,000,000 |
| Marketing and listings | 10% | 100,000,000 |
| Reserve | 10% | 100,000,000 |

The proposed team allocation has a 12-month cliff and a 36-month total vesting duration. The vesting wallet must be funded separately after deployment.

## Transparency and launch requirements

The token contract has no blacklist, transfer taxes, pause control, or upgrade mechanism. Before a mainnet launch, the project intends to publish verified BscScan source, an independent audit and remediation status, a disclosed liquidity lock, and Safe Multisig treasury controls. Completion of these items must be verified through official records; this draft does not claim they are complete.

If adopted after legal review, a buyback policy may use **[__%] of net subscription revenue**. This is an unfilled placeholder, not a commitment. Any approved policy should publish its exact calculation, approvals, execution addresses, and transaction records. Buybacks and burns do not guarantee price effects or financial returns.
