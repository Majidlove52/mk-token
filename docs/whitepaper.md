# MK Alpha (MKA) Whitepaper

**Status:** Working draft. Product details, dates, personnel, commercial terms, and links marked as placeholders require review before publication.

## Abstract

MK Alpha describes a proposed ecosystem of trading-related software tools, including indicators, market scanners, and signal applications. MKA is a fixed-supply utility token intended to support optional access and payment features in that ecosystem. Product availability and token utility depend on development, legal review, and operational readiness. This document is informational and does not promise any financial result, token liquidity, exchange listing, or product outcome.

## Problem

People who use multiple market tools may encounter fragmented interfaces, separate subscriptions, and inconsistent access controls. A common product layer could make it easier to discover tools and manage access. MK Alpha is a proposed approach to that product problem; it does not remove the financial, technical, or market risks associated with trading or digital assets.

## Solution

The proposed MK ecosystem brings together:

- **Indicators:** configurable technical views for supported markets and intervals.
- **Scanners:** filters that help users examine market data against selected criteria.
- **Signal apps:** software that presents generated or third-party signals with clear methodology and risk context.

Tools are informational software, not individualized financial advice. Users remain responsible for independent research and decisions. Supported features, data sources, performance characteristics, and availability will be documented as products are released.

## MKA utility

Proposed utility, subject to product delivery and legal review, includes:

- **Subscription discounts:** optional discounts for eligible MK Alpha subscriptions, under terms published by the product operator.
- **Staking tiers:** locking MKA to qualify for product access levels. A tier is an access rule, not a promise of yield, profit, or token rewards.
- **In-app payments:** paying for supported products or services where permitted.
- **Token-gated tools:** access checks for selected indicators, scanners, or signal applications.

These features are proposals and are not implemented by the MKA token contract. Any staking or payment feature will require separate terms, technical review, and appropriate legal analysis before release.

## Security and transparency

The MKA token contract has no post-deployment mint function, blacklist, transfer taxes, pause control, or upgrade mechanism. These are statements about the token contract only and do not guarantee that surrounding services or third-party systems are risk-free.

Before any mainnet launch, the project intends to publish verified source on BscScan, obtain and publish an independent audit and remediation status, lock liquidity for a disclosed period, and operate treasury funds through a Safe Multisig. These are launch requirements, not claims that those steps are already complete. Publish addresses, allocation movements, vesting status, material security findings, and incident updates through official channels.
